You can delete your Leave26 account and all associated data at any time.

## Option 1: In the app

1. Open Leave26 and tap your **avatar** in the top right corner.
2. Scroll to **Account** and tap **Delete account**.
3. Confirm and verify with your Google account.

Your account and all data are deleted immediately.

## Option 2: Without the app

Send an e-mail to **leave26@gmail.com** with the subject **"Delete Leave26 account"** from the e-mail address of your Google account that you use for Leave26.

We will delete your account within **30 days** and confirm the deletion by e-mail.

## What is deleted

- your Leave26 account (sign-in with Google)
- all app data: checkouts, history, routes, 180s, goals, profile name and avatar

Residual copies in technical backups of our service provider (Google Firebase) are deleted according to its retention periods. Your Google account itself is **not** affected.
